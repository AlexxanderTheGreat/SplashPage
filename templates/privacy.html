Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Morales Event & Party Rentals is a family-owned event rental business serving Monterey County, California. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book with us (on our website, on Facebook or Instagram, by phone, text or WhatsApp), we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email, event date, event address or city, guest count, and the details of what you need.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your request, send you a quote, deliver and set up your rental, collect payment, and contact you about your event. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services we use to run our business, and only as needed:

  • Formspree, which delivers our website quote form to our email.
  • Email, calendar and payment providers (such as Google and Square).
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), when you contact us or submit a form through their apps, and for advertising measurement (see below).

Cookies and advertising

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code that helps us understand which of our Facebook and Instagram ads bring visitors and quote requests. It may use cookies and collect information such as pages visited and form submissions. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed for your booking and for our business and tax records, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you. California residents have these rights under state law.
